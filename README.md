### Hi, I'm Ali 👋

R&D staff member at **Oak Ridge National Laboratory**, where I use machine learning and signal processing to detect and locate problems on the power grid. I also teach at the **University of Tennessee, Knoxville**. PhD in Computer Engineering, IEEE Senior Member.

Before ORNL I spent eight years as computer engineering faculty at İstanbul Ticaret University, where I founded [Tapir Labs](https://tapirlab.com).

**What I work on**
- **Grid sensing & ML**: anomaly detection, explainable event diagnosis, real-time monitoring
- **Embedded & low-power**: STM32U5 (Cortex-M33), C, FreeRTOS, power gating and stop modes. My master's thesis was a microkernel OS for embedded systems, so this goes back a while.
- **Wireless, IoT & security**: V2X, visible light communication, spectrum sensing, password policy
- **Infrastructure**: self-hosted home lab, on-prem LLM serving
